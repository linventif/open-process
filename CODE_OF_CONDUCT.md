# Code of Conduct

Open Process welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. This code
applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed
community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience, especially newcomers.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and changes; explain disagreements.
- Respect different experiences, accessibility needs and language abilities.
- Accept corrections and respect other people's boundaries and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption and publishing private information without
permission are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern is
also unacceptable.

## Report a concern

Email [contact@open-process.linv.dev](mailto:contact@open-process.linv.dev) with
the subject `Open Process community concern`. Include links or relevant context
and explain how you may be contacted. You do not need to confront the person
involved or publish your report in an issue.

Reports should be handled discreetly, sharing information only as needed to
assess and address the situation. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
If a report concerns a maintainer, identify that conflict in the report; the
project does not currently provide an independent appeals body.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request a correction, warn a participant, remove content, lock a
conversation or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, according to
the severity and recurrence of the behavior. They should explain decisions to
the people involved when doing so is appropriate and protects those affected.
Participants can ask for reconsideration through the same private contact.
