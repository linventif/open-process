THE WHOLE PICTURE

Less tool switching.
More understanding.

The diagram, the maths and the discovery belong together. So we put them together.

01 — MODEL
◇→▢

Make the flow visible.

Build BPMN 2.0 diagrams with pools, lanes, events and gateways. Drag elements into place and catch modelling issues as you go.

  • BPMN 2.0 XML import & export
  • BPMN ↔ Petri net conversion
  • Light, dark & hand-drawn styles
02 — ANALYSE
⊙→▯→○

Go beyond the diagram.

Play the tokens, inspect the matrices and explore reachable states. Find out how your Petri net actually behaves.

  • Pre, Post & incidence matrices
  • Invariants, deadlocks & soundness
  • PNML, LaTeX & CSV exports
03 — DISCOVER
ABCD→α

Let the traces speak.

Start with an event log. Explore the relations, follow the Alpha algorithm and turn observed behaviour into a Petri net.

  • Text, CSV & XES event logs
  • Footprint matrix & Alpha steps
  • Replay traces on the discovered net

Explore the learning tools↗
  1. 01

    Work it out yourself.

    Fill in Pre, Post and incidence matrices, or build your footprint matrix and Alpha sets by hand.

  2. 02

    Check your reasoning.

    Compare your answers, spot missing or extra items, and use hints to move forward.

  3. 03

    See the result come alive.

    Generate a net from your answers. Replay the traces, fire transitions and explore the analysis alongside your model.
